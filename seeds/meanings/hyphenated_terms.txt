# From the hand-written study notes. Format: entry | meaning
24/7 | All the time, without stopping: twenty-four hours a day, seven days a week. Also used before a noun: a 24/7 store.
bonkers | Crazy or wildly silly; also, extremely excited or angry, as in "go bonkers."
bottom up | Starting from the lowest level, the details or ordinary people, and building toward the whole, rather than starting from the top.
brother in arms | A fellow soldier who fought beside you; also, a close ally who struggles with you for the same cause.
frog-march | To force someone to walk forward by holding their arms tightly, often behind their back, as police or guards do.
go round | One round, turn or attempt at something, often one of a repeated series, as in "the second go-round" or "another go-round."
god-fearing | Deeply religious and trying to live a good, moral life according to one's faith.
ham-fisted | Clumsy and lacking skill or care, especially in handling a situation or using your hands.
high-wire act | A risky situation that needs great skill and careful balance, where one mistake could cause failure.
letdown | A disappointment; something that turns out much less good or exciting than you hoped.
off color | Slightly rude or offensive, usually about sex or bodily functions; in poor taste.
one-eighty | A complete change to the opposite opinion, direction or behavior; usually in 'do a one-eighty'.
one size fits all | Meant to suit everyone or every situation in the same way, often when it really does not fit everyone's needs.
push and pull | A struggle between two opposite forces, wishes or groups, each trying to move things in its own direction.
shoutout | A public mention of someone to thank, praise or say hello to them.
spaced out | Not paying attention, as if your mind is far away; dazed.
spot on | Exactly right or accurate.
Stone Age | The earliest period of human history, when people made tools from stone; informally, a time or way of doing things that seems extremely old-fashioned.
try hard | A person who tries too hard to impress others or to fit in, so that it looks forced.
upper crust | The richest and most powerful social class.
wound up | Tense, nervous or overexcited, so that it is hard to relax.
x-ray | A picture of the inside of the body, such as bones, made with a special kind of radiation; also, to take such a picture.
