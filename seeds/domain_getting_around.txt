# Expressions
Can you give me a ride? | Asking someone to drive you somewhere.
I'll pick you up at seven. | Offering to come get someone in your car at a set time.
Can you drop me off here? | Asking a driver to stop and let you out.
Where should I park? | Asking where it's allowed or best to leave your car.
Is there parking nearby? | Asking whether you can park close to a place.
How far is it? | Asking the distance to a place.
How long does it take to get there? | Asking the travel time.
Is it within walking distance? | Asking whether you can walk there easily.
Take a left at the light. | Directions: turn left at the traffic light.
Go straight for two blocks. | Directions: continue ahead past two street sections.
It's on your right. | Directions: the place will be on your right side.
You can't miss it. | Saying a place is very easy to find.
Traffic is terrible. | Complaining about heavy traffic.
I'm stuck in traffic. | Saying you are delayed because traffic isn't moving.
I'm running late. | Saying you will arrive later than planned.
I'm on my way. | Saying you have left and are coming.
I'll be there in ten minutes. | Telling someone when you will arrive.
Fill it up, please. | Asking a gas station attendant to fill the tank completely; you hear it mainly in New Jersey and parts of Oregon, where attendants pump the gas.
I got pulled over. | Saying a police officer stopped your car.
License and registration, please. | What a police officer asks a driver for during a traffic stop.
Does this bus go downtown? | Asking whether a bus goes to the city center.
Which stop do I get off at? | Asking where to leave the bus or train.
Is this seat taken? | Asking whether you can sit in an empty seat.
Window or aisle? | An airline agent asking which seat you prefer.
Do you have any bags to check? | An airline agent asking whether you have luggage for the plane's hold.
My flight got delayed. | Saying your flight will leave later than scheduled.
I missed my connection. | Saying you didn't reach your connecting flight in time.
Where's baggage claim? | Asking where to collect your checked luggage at the airport.
