# Expressions
Hello, this is Michael. | Answering the phone or starting a call by saying your name.
Hi, may I speak to Sarah? | Asking for a specific person on the phone.
Speaking. | Saying "I'm the person you asked for."
Who's calling, please? | Asking the caller's name.
Hold on a second. | Asking the caller to wait briefly.
Can I put you on hold? | Asking permission to make the caller wait.
Thanks for holding. | Thanking the caller for waiting.
Let me transfer you. | Saying you will connect the caller to another person or department.
She's not available right now. | Saying the person can't come to the phone.
Can I take a message? | Offering to write down a message for someone.
Could you have him call me back? | Asking for the person to return your call.
What's the best number to reach you? | Asking which phone number to use to call someone back.
Sorry, I didn't catch that. | Saying you didn't hear or understand.
Could you say that again? | Asking someone to repeat.
Could you speak a little louder? | Asking someone to speak up because you can't hear well.
Could you spell your last name? | Asking someone to spell their name letter by letter.
Let me read that back to you. | Repeating information to confirm it's correct.
You're breaking up. | Saying the call quality is bad and you can't hear parts.
I think we got cut off. | Saying the call ended by accident.
I'm calling about my order. | Stating the reason for your call.
I'm returning your call. | Saying you are calling back after someone called you.
Is now a good time? | Asking whether the other person can talk now.
I'll let you go. | Politely ending a call.
Thanks for calling. | Ending a call you received.
Talk to you later. | A casual goodbye on the phone.
Sorry, wrong number. | Saying the caller dialed the wrong person.
I'll text you the address. | Offering to send information by text message.
Just shoot me a text. | Casual: send me a text message.
Can I call you back? | Asking to continue the call later.
I'll call you right back. | Promising to call again in a moment.
