# Expressions
Let's get started. | Opening a meeting.
Thanks, everyone, for joining. | A polite way to begin a meeting.
Let's go around the room. | Asking each person to speak in turn.
Let's do a quick round of introductions. | Asking each person to introduce themselves briefly.
Can everyone see my screen? | Checking that a shared screen is visible on a video call.
I'll share my screen. | Saying you will show your screen to everyone on the call.
You're on mute. | Telling someone on a call that their microphone is off.
Sorry, I was on mute. | Apologizing for speaking without your microphone on.
Can you hear me okay? | Checking your audio on a call.
Go ahead. | Inviting someone to speak first.
Sorry, go ahead. | Said when two people start talking at once, letting the other speak.
Could you say more about that? | Asking someone to explain further.
Just to make sure I understand… | Starting a summary to check your understanding.
I'd like to add something. | Asking to contribute a point.
Building on what Maria said… | Connecting your point to someone else's.
That's a fair point. | Acknowledging that someone is right about something.
I see it a bit differently. | Politely disagreeing.
I'm not sure that's the case. | Politely doubting a statement.
Let's take this offline. | Suggesting discussing a topic later with fewer people.
Let's circle back to that. | Suggesting returning to a topic later.
We're running out of time. | Saying the meeting must end soon.
Let's wrap up. | Starting to end the meeting.
What are the next steps? | Asking what will happen after the meeting and who will do it.
Who's taking the lead on this? | Asking who is responsible for a task.
I'll follow up by email. | Saying you will send more information after the meeting.
I'll send out the notes. | Offering to send a summary of the meeting.
Does that work for everyone? | Checking that a plan or time is acceptable to all.
Works for me. | Agreeing to a proposal or time.
I have a hard stop at three. | Saying you must leave at exactly 3 o'clock.
Can we push this to next week? | Asking to move a meeting to a later date.
Sorry I'm late. | Apologizing for joining after the start.
I have to drop off. | Saying you must leave a call early.
Thanks, everyone. Talk soon. | A common way to end a call or meeting.
