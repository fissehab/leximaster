# meaning: REK-erd (noun) and rih-KAWRD (verb), tair (rip) and teer (from the
# eye). Each entry shows every sound with its own recording, and each meaning
# is marked with the sound it takes. Read by build_dataset.assemble() through
# tools/heteronyms.py; `.venv/bin/python tools/heteronyms.py report` shows
# which sound every meaning got.
#
#   key | applies to | IPA | respelling | short gloss | Commons recording (optional)
#
# "applies to" is one or more parts of speech ("noun; adjective"), each of
# which may carry "~ regex" to pick meanings by their definition ("noun ~
# drop|eyes"); "*" is everything left. A meaning takes the first sound whose
# regex matches it, else the first whose parts of speech include it. The
# entry leads with (shows the IPA of, plays first) the sound of its main
# meaning, the study notes' core definition; else the first listed.
# The recording names a Commons file in data/cache/commons_us.json, an
# American speaker saying that form ("record-verb"); without one the sound is
# synthesized from its IPA (tools/tts.py).
