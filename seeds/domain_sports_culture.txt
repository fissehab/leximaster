# Expressions
Who are you rooting for? | Asking which team someone supports in a game.
Did you catch the game last night? | Asking whether someone watched a recent game.
What a game! | Saying a game was exciting.
That was a close one. | Saying the game or result was very close.
They got crushed. | Casual: a team lost badly.
They pulled it off. | Saying a team managed to win despite difficulty.
It went into overtime. | Saying a game was tied at the end and played extra time.
I'm not really a sports person. | Saying politely that you don't follow sports.
I'm a huge Lakers fan. | Saying you strongly support a team.
Have you seen it yet? | Asking whether someone has watched a show or movie.
No spoilers! | Asking people not to reveal what happens in a show or movie.
I'm hooked. | Casual: you can't stop watching something.
I binged the whole season. | Saying you watched all the episodes in a short time.
What are you watching these days? | A common small-talk question about TV shows.
It's worth watching. | Recommending a show or movie.
It was overrated. | Saying something was not as good as people say.
I'm not caught up yet. | Saying you haven't watched the latest episodes.
Are you going to the concert? | Asking whether someone will attend a concert.
It sold out in minutes. | Saying tickets were all bought very quickly.
Did you see that video? | Asking whether someone saw something online.
It went viral. | Saying something spread quickly online.
Happy Thanksgiving! | A greeting on the US holiday in late November.
Any plans for the Fourth? | Asking about plans for the Independence Day holiday on July 4.
