# Expressions
I'm just looking, thanks. | Telling a sales associate you don't need help yet.
Do you have this in a medium? | Asking whether the item comes in another size.
Do you have this in black? | Asking whether the item comes in another color.
Where are the fitting rooms? | Asking where you can try on clothes.
It doesn't fit. | Saying the clothing is the wrong size.
It's a little tight. | Saying the clothing is slightly too small.
How much is this? | Asking the price.
Is this on sale? | Asking if the item is discounted.
Do you price match? | Asking if the store will lower its price to match a competitor's.
Can I get a gift receipt? | Asking for a receipt without prices so the recipient can exchange the gift.
I'd like to return this. | Telling the cashier you want to give an item back for a refund.
I'd like to exchange this for a different size. | Asking to swap an item rather than get your money back.
Do you need a bag? | The cashier asking if you want a bag for your items.
Paper or plastic? | The cashier asking which kind of bag you want.
Debit or credit? | The cashier asking which kind of card you're using.
Would you like your receipt? | The cashier asking if you want a printed receipt.
Do you have a rewards card? | Asking if you belong to the store's loyalty program.
Can you hold this for me? | Asking the store to keep an item for you to pick up later.
Do you know when it'll be back in stock? | Asking when a sold-out item will be available again.
What's your return policy? | Asking how long you have to return an item and what you get back: a refund, an exchange or store credit.
That's a steal. | Casual: a very good price.
That's a rip-off. | Casual: far too expensive.
I'll think about it. | Politely saying you won't buy now.
Where can I find the batteries? | Asking a store worker where an item is located.
Which aisle is the cereal in? | Asking for the aisle number of a product.
Is this line open? | Asking whether a checkout lane is taking customers.
