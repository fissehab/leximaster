# Expressions
I'm so excited! | Saying you're very happy about something coming up.
I can't wait! | Saying you're very eager for something to happen.
That made my day. | Something small made you very happy.
I'm a little stressed out. | Saying you're feeling pressure or anxiety.
I've had a long day. | Saying you're tired after a busy or difficult day.
I'm wiped out. | Casual: very tired.
I'm not feeling it today. | Casual: you lack energy or motivation today.
I'm kind of bummed. | Casual: a little sad or disappointed.
That's a bummer. | Casual: that's disappointing.
That really bugs me. | Casual: that annoys me.
It gets on my nerves. | It irritates me, especially when repeated.
I'm over it. | Casual: I no longer care about it, or I'm tired of it.
I'm fed up. | Saying you've had enough of a situation.
I'm so relieved. | Saying a worry has gone away.
I'm proud of you. | Praising someone for an achievement.
I feel bad about that. | Saying you feel guilty or sorry.
No hard feelings. | Saying you're not angry about what happened.
Don't take it personally. | Telling someone a criticism or event isn't about them.
I didn't mean it like that. | Saying your words were misunderstood.
Are you okay? | Checking on someone who seems upset.
What's wrong? | Asking why someone seems upset.
Do you want to talk about it? | Offering to listen to someone's problem.
I'm here for you. | Offering emotional support.
Hang in there. | Encouraging someone going through a hard time.
You've got this. | Encouraging someone before a challenge.
It'll be okay. | Reassuring someone.
That must be hard. | Showing empathy for someone's difficulty.
I know how you feel. | Saying you understand someone's feelings.
Take a deep breath. | Telling someone to calm down.
I'm on cloud nine. | Casual: extremely happy.
