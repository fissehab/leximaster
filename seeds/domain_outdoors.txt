# Expressions
Is it safe to swim here? | Asking whether the water or currents are dangerous.
The water's freezing! | Casual: the water is very cold.
The water's perfect. | The water temperature is pleasant for swimming.
Watch out for the riptide. | A warning about a strong current that pulls swimmers away from shore.
Did you put on sunscreen? | Reminding someone to protect their skin from the sun.
I'm getting burned. | Saying your skin is starting to get sunburned.
Let's find some shade. | Suggesting moving out of the direct sun.
Want to go for a swim? | Inviting someone to swim.
Want to go for a walk on the beach? | Inviting someone to walk along the shore.
It's a beautiful day. | Common small talk about good weather.
It's so humid today. | Complaining that the air feels damp and heavy.
It's supposed to rain later. | Saying the forecast predicts rain.
It's really coming down. | Casual: it is raining heavily.
Looks like it's clearing up. | Saying the clouds or rain are going away.
How long is the hike? | Asking the distance or time a trail takes.
Is the trail marked? | Asking whether there are signs along the path.
What a view! | Admiring scenery.
We should head back before it gets dark. | Suggesting returning before night.
Do you need a parking pass? | Asking whether you must pay or register to park.
Are dogs allowed? | Asking whether pets are permitted.
I'm so ready for vacation. | Casual: you really need a break.
We're just taking it easy this weekend. | Saying you plan to relax, without big plans.
We had a blast. | Casual: we had a great time.
How was your trip? | Common question after someone returns from travel.
It was so relaxing. | Saying a trip or day helped you rest.
