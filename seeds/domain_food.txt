# Expressions
This is delicious. | A simple, natural way to praise food.
This hits the spot. | Said when food is exactly what you wanted or needed.
It's a little too salty for me. | A polite way to say the food has too much salt.
It could use a little more salt. | A gentle way to say the food needs more seasoning.
I'm not much of a cook. | Saying modestly that you don't cook well or often.
What's in this? | Asking about the ingredients of a dish.
Did you make this from scratch? | Asking if someone cooked it themselves from basic ingredients.
I'm trying to eat healthy. | Explaining that you are choosing healthier food.
I'm watching my carbs. | Saying you are limiting bread, pasta, sugar and similar foods.
I have a sweet tooth. | Saying you love sweet foods.
I could eat. | Casual: "I'm a little hungry and happy to eat."
I'm starving. | Casual exaggeration: very hungry.
I'm full. | Saying you have eaten enough.
I couldn't eat another bite. | Saying you are completely full.
Help yourself. | Inviting someone to take as much food as they want.
Dig in! | Telling people to start eating.
Would you like seconds? | Offering another serving of the same food.
It's an acquired taste. | Something most people only like after trying it several times.
It's not really my thing. | A polite way to say you don't like a food.
That smells amazing. | Praise for food that is cooking or being served.
