# Expressions
How's it going? | A casual greeting; "Good, you?" is the usual reply, not a long answer.
How's your day going? | A friendly greeting from a cashier, coworker or neighbor.
What have you been up to? | Asking what someone has been doing lately.
What are you up to this weekend? | Asking about someone's weekend plans.
Did you do anything fun this weekend? | A common Monday question to start a conversation.
Not much, you? | A casual reply that turns the question back to the other person.
Same old, same old. | Casual: nothing has changed; life is as usual.
I can't complain. | Casual: things are fine.
Hanging in there. | Things are a bit hard, but I'm managing.
So, what do you do? | Asking what someone's job is; very common when meeting someone new.
How do you know the host? | A common question at parties to start a conversation with a stranger.
Where are you from originally? | Asking where someone grew up.
How long have you lived here? | Asking how long someone has been in the area.
Have you been here before? | Asking about a place: a restaurant, city or event.
That sounds like fun. | A friendly response showing interest in someone's plans.
That's awesome! | Casual, enthusiastic praise.
Oh, nice! | A short, friendly reaction to good news.
No way! | Casual: I'm surprised; I can't believe it.
Good for you! | Showing you're happy about someone's success.
That's too bad. | Showing sympathy for small bad news.
I'm sorry to hear that. | Showing sympathy for bad news.
Tell me more. | Inviting someone to keep talking about a topic.
How did that go? | Asking about the result of something someone did.
It was great seeing you. | A friendly way to end a conversation.
We should get together sometime. | A friendly suggestion to meet again; often polite rather than a firm plan.
Let me know if you're ever in town. | An invitation to meet if the person visits your city.
I should get going. | A polite way to say you need to leave.
I'll let you go. | A polite way to end a conversation, implying the other person is busy.
Nice chatting with you. | A friendly way to end a short conversation.
Say hi to your family for me. | Asking someone to pass on your greetings.
Thanks for having me. | Thanking a host when you leave a party or visit.
Make yourself at home. | A host telling a guest to relax and feel comfortable.
Can I get you anything? | A host offering a guest a drink or food.
Long time no see! | A casual greeting to someone you haven't seen for a long time.
What a small world! | Said when you discover a surprising connection with someone.
